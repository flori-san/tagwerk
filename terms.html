2. What Tagwerk does

Tagwerk plans your strength training and runs or swims and sets your next weights and paces from what you logged. It counts your food against a daily kcal and protein target, keeps a short list of daily habits, shows your weight trend, and has an AI coach that writes notes and a Sunday review and answers your questions. The coach can suggest changes to your program or numbers; nothing changes until you tap Apply.
