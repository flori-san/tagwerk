Help

Something confusing, broken or missing? Email hello@stonefield.app. A screenshot helps. You'll get an answer from the person who made the app.

Finding things

  • The screens: Overview, Training, Eating and Habits. Swipe left and right to move between them.
  • Account is to the left of Overview: swipe right from the first screen.
  • Ask the coach is at the top right of every screen.

Tagwerk in Claude

You can connect Tagwerk to Claude and ask about your day, your training and your weight, or log a meal or a weight by just saying it ("log 250 g of skyr with blueberries"). In Claude, add the Tagwerk connector, then sign in with the same Apple ID you use in the app and choose Allow. Meals and weights logged in Claude show in Tagwerk the next time you open it. To disconnect, open tagwerk.stonefield.app/connect.html.

Apple Health

Tagwerk reads your weight, body fat, lean body mass and steps. Your scale's app sends weigh-ins to Apple Health, and Tagwerk picks them up. If you said Not now in the welcome, connect it in Account. If numbers are missing, check Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Tagwerk and turn the types on. No scale? Log your weight on Overview.

Training

Tagwerk sets your next weight from the sets you ticked and whether your form was good. Weights go up slowly on purpose; every sixth week is lighter. Move a session to another day anytime on Training. To change the program or your numbers, ask the coach: nothing changes until you tap Apply.

Food

Take a photo of a meal, ideally with a few words ("chicken, rice, a spoon of oil"), or type what you ate. The kcal and protein are an AI's estimate; correct them if you know better, and your own foods are remembered.

Runs

Allow location "While Using the App". The run keeps measuring with the screen locked, and the voice cues play over your music.

Free week and subscription

  • The free week: 7 days of everything, no account needed. It ends by itself and never turns into a paid subscription. Account shows the days left.
  • After that: €10 a week or €100 a year, paid through your Apple ID. It renews until you cancel.
  • Cancel: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Tagwerk, at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are Apple's: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • New phone? Sign in with Apple, then Restore purchases on the paywall or in Account.
  • Keep your data past the free week or across phones: Account › Sign in with Apple.

Deleting your account

Account › Delete account. Everything goes straight away, meal photos included. Deleting the account doesn't cancel a subscription: cancel it with Apple as above. Can't open the app? Email us and we'll delete it for you. See the privacy policy for what's kept and where, and the terms of use for the rules.

Not medical advice

Tagwerk is a training, food and habit tracker. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Stop a session and see a doctor if you have chest pain, dizziness, breathlessness out of proportion to effort, or pain that doesn't ease with rest. It's not for injuries, rehab, pregnancy or medical conditions, or anyone for whom counting food feels unhealthy.
