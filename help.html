Help

Something confusing, broken or missing? Email florian.schirnick@gmail.com. A screenshot helps. You'll get an answer from the person who made the app.

Finding things

  • The screens: Overview, Training, Eating and Habits. Swipe left and right to move between them.
  • Account is to the left of Overview: swipe right from the first screen.
  • Ask the coach is at the top right of every screen.

Apple Health

Tagwerk reads your weight, body fat, lean body mass and steps. Your scale's app sends weigh-ins to Apple Health, and Tagwerk picks them up. If you said Not now in the welcome, connect it in Account. If numbers are missing, check Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Tagwerk and turn the types on. No scale? Log your weight on Overview.

Training

Tagwerk sets your next weight from the sets you ticked and whether your form was good. Weights go up slowly on purpose; every sixth week is lighter. Move a session to another day anytime on Training. To change the program or your numbers, ask the coach: nothing changes until you tap Apply.

Food

Take a photo of a meal, ideally with a few words ("chicken, rice, a spoon of oil"), or type what you ate. The kcal and protein are an AI's estimate; correct them if you know better, and your own foods are remembered.

Runs

Allow location "While Using the App". The run keeps measuring with the screen locked, and the voice cues play over your music.

Deleting your account

Account › Delete account. Everything goes straight away, meal photos included. See the privacy policy for what's kept and where.

Not medical advice

Tagwerk is a training, food and habit tracker. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Stop a session and see a doctor if you have chest pain, dizziness, breathlessness out of proportion to effort, or pain that doesn't ease with rest. It's not for injuries, rehab, pregnancy or medical conditions, or anyone for whom counting food feels unhealthy.
