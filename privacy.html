Privacy policy

Last changed 10/11/2026

Tagwerk keeps what you log so it can coach you. It's never sold, never used for ads, and nobody tracks you across other apps or websites.

Who is responsible

Tagwerk is built by Stonefield Sp. z o.o., ul. Śniadeckich 10C/33, 60-774 Poznań, Poland, which is responsible for your data (the "controller"). Questions or requests: florian.schirnick@gmail.com.

What Tagwerk keeps

  • Your account: an account number. The free week starts without signing in, as a guest with only that number. If you sign in with Apple, also the name and email Apple shares (the email can be Apple's private relay address).
  • Your purchases: when your free week started, and which subscription you have and when it ends. When you buy, the App Store gives the app a record of the purchase signed by Apple (the product, its dates and a transaction number); Tagwerk's server checks Apple's signature and keeps only the facts above. Payment is Apple's: Tagwerk never sees your card or bank details.
  • What you enter: your name, sex, age, height and the notes you give the coach; your food targets and meals, meal photos and typed meals; your habits; your training (sessions, sets, weights and reps); your runs and swims, including the GPS route of a run; your weigh-ins and weight goal; and your chats with the coach.
  • From Apple Health, only if you allow it: weight, body fat, lean body mass and steps. Tagwerk only reads; it never writes to Apple Health.
  • Location, only while a run is going, to measure distance and pace. Not at any other time.
  • Your voice, only while you use the voice button on Eating. Apple's speech recognition turns it into words in the meal field, on your phone when your phone can; the words are kept like typed ones, the sound is not kept or sent to Tagwerk.

Tagwerk has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools inside it.

Why

Only to run the app for you: to plan your training and set your next weights and paces, to count your food against your targets, to show your progress, and for the coach's notes, reviews and answers; and to know whether your free week or subscription is running. Nothing else.

Where it's kept

On Supabase (a database service) in Frankfurt, Germany. Your data is private to your account: the database only lets your own signed-in account read or change it. It travels encrypted between your phone and the server.

Who else sees it

  • Google's Gemini (an AI). To estimate kcal and protein and to write the coach's notes and answers, Tagwerk's server sends Gemini what it needs for that request: a meal's photo or words, your training, weight and steps, and what you ask the coach. Google processes it for Tagwerk under its paid API terms, which don't allow it to use your data to train its models. You agree to this in the welcome before anything is sent.
  • Apple handles sign-in (Sign in with Apple) and payment for the subscription.
  • Supabase stores the data, as above.

Nobody else. Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used for marketing. Data from Apple Health is used only for your coaching.

Health data

Weight, body fat, food and training are health data. Tagwerk uses them only with your explicit consent: the "I agree" in the welcome and Apple Health's own permission screen. You can take it back anytime: turn off Tagwerk's access in the Health app (Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices), or delete your account.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

As long as you have an account. In the app, swipe right from the first screen to Account, then Delete account: everything Tagwerk holds about you, meal photos included, is deleted straight away and Apple's sign-in link to Tagwerk is removed. You can also clear just your meal history there. A subscription is with Apple and keeps renewing until you cancel it in your App Store settings (Settings › your name › Subscriptions); Apple keeps its own record of your purchases.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, or to limit or object to how it's used. Email florian.schirnick@gmail.com and you'll get an answer within a month. If you live in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Age

Tagwerk is for adults. It's not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. If a change affects what is shared or with whom, the app asks you again first.
